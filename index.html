Our Mission

Demystifying Skincare Through Spatial Computing

SkinSpace AR was founded on a simple conviction: skincare consumers deserve objective, science-based clarity rather than opaque marketing claims and miracle promises.

By combining Web AR standards with dermatological science, SkinSpace bridges the gap between ingredient lists and biological function. You can physically observe how humectants bond with water molecules and how ceramides interlock to form the stratum corneum barrier.

How Web AR Marker Tracking Works

SkinSpace utilizes high-contrast fiducial markers (the standardized Hiro pattern) with computer vision edge detection. When your smartphone camera detects the dark border and geometric matrix, it computes the exact 6-degree-of-freedom (6DOF) spatial pose, anchoring 3D models seamlessly to your real table or desk without requiring app store downloads.
